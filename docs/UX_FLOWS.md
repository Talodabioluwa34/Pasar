# Pasar – UX Flows (MVP)

## 1. Entry Point

### App Launch
- Default entry: Marketplace feed
- No forced onboarding
- Trust-first banners visible immediately

---

## 2. Buyer Flow (Happy Path)

1. Open app
2. Browse phone listings
3. View product detail
4. See trust signals:
   - Escrow protected
   - IMEI verified
   - Seller reputation
5. Click “Buy”
6. Select payment method (NGN)
7. Confirm escrow lock
8. Wait for delivery
9. Inspect phone
10. Accept delivery
11. Transaction completes

---

## 3. Buyer Flow (Dispute Path)

1. Delivery received
2. Buyer taps “Report Issue”
3. Upload evidence
4. Escrow frozen
5. Pasar reviews
6. Resolution:
   - Refund buyer
   - Or release seller funds

---

## 4. Seller Flow (Listing)

1. Seller signs in
2. Create listing
3. Upload phone video
4. Enter IMEI
5. System verifies
6. Listing goes live

---

## 5. Logistics Flow

1. Seller requests pickup
2. Rider verifies item
3. OTP handoff
4. Delivery tracking
5. Buyer confirmation

---

## 6. Non-Goals (Explicit)

- No social feeds
- No chats outside transaction
- No multi-category browsing
- No crypto UI exposure

---

**End of UX Flows**
